## Process
1. A dumpster, by day, in an industrial alley with a lot of graffitis, seems like it is in the US
2. parse image with exif tool : inconclusive
3. google > the WM sticker on the dumpster : confirmed WM is an american company
4. google image > "goof.t" graffiti : found multiple occurences (reddit, facebook, instagram) with exact "GOOF.TROOP" graffiti
   On FB found a post tagged "washington DC" with an exact image tagged "Union Market District"
5. googlemaps > washington DC union market district : streetview on a alley seamingly approriate
   some building have changed, confirmed graffiti with wall textures and brick details
